US, Russian and Ukrainian officials held a second and final scheduled day of peace talks in Abu Dhabi, with no breakthrough reported and drone debris killing 2 people in Russia's Smolensk region overnight (Reuters).
The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted Houthi missiles fired at Riyadh on Wednesday, as tanker attacks continued near the Strait of Hormuz, where the IMO has tracked 131 incidents since 28 February (Reuters).
At the COP31 summit in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres told leaders "the climate crisis is outrunning us," as host Turkey pushed a deal to lift electricity's share of global energy use to 35% by 2035; the US is absent for a second year (Reuters).
Spain staged its first general strike in 14 years over housing: a deficit of about 750,000 homes, with average rent of 1,176 euros equal to 98.7% of a young person's monthly salary (Reuters).
A Reuters investigation found China's Northwestern Polytechnical University, blacklisted by the US since 2001 over PLA links, bought two Nvidia H200 AI chips in October for about $123,000 through a domestic supplier (Reuters).
Sudan recalled its ambassador from Ethiopia and accused Addis Ababa and the UAE of involvement in drone attacks, a claim neither government confirmed (Al Jazeera).
The UN migration agency warned almost 900,000 people in Kenya could be displaced this year, mostly by flooding, as forecasters put the chance of a very strong El Nino at 81% (IOM via BBC).
Pope Leo ended a three-day Argentina tour with an outdoor Mass at the Lujan shrine and flew on to Peru, where he was once a bishop (Reuters).
China's Wang Yi told Cuba's foreign minister in Beijing that China will firmly support Cuba against sanctions and external interference (Xinhua via Reuters).
Australia's weather bureau warned of giant hail and flash flooding in Queensland and New South Wales, with a 96 km/h gust recorded at Brisbane Airport (Bureau of Meteorology).
